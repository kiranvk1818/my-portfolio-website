what it is

healthcare, without the wait.

AarohCare is a healthcare booking platform. Patients book outpatient appointments at clinics, hospitals and labs, and follow their queue token live instead of sitting in a crowded waiting room.

Providers run their queues, doctors and lab reports from an admin site. A superadmin panel looks after every provider, user, ad, payment and emergency message.

I co-founded AarohCare and lead marketing. I also work on product with the team, from the patient app and admin screens to the showcase website, pitch deck and clinic onboarding material.

what is inside

  • Patient app for booking OP appointments
  • Live token tracking, so patients know where they are in the queue
  • Admin site for clinics, hospitals and labs
  • Superadmin panel for the whole platform
  • Showcase website, pitch deck and onboarding templates
