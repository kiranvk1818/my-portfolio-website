privacy.

This privacy page explains the limited information handling for the Kiran V K portfolio at kiranvk.netlify.app. The site is primarily a static portfolio containing information about Kiran's work, projects and ways to make contact.

Information you provide

If you email Kiran, your message and email address are received in the normal operation of the email service. They are used to read and respond to your request, discuss possible work, or maintain a professional conversation. Do not send sensitive personal information through the contact email.

Information collected by the site

The website does not require an account, does not ask for a form submission and does not intentionally collect a profile about visitors. The theme preference is stored locally in your browser when you use the theme control. Hosting providers may process standard technical request data such as an IP address, user agent and requested URL for delivery, security and reliability.

Third parties and choices

The site links to LinkedIn, GitHub, AarohCare and SaveMyLink. Those services have their own policies. You can avoid local theme storage by clearing site data or using your browser's privacy controls. Questions about this page can be sent to kiranjayan18@gmail.com.
